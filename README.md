# Hyeokjae Kwon, M.D., Ph.D. — Academic Website

Official academic website and portfolio of **Dr. Hyeokjae Kwon, M.D., Ph.D.**
- **Visiting Professor**, Massachusetts General Hospital (MGH) & Harvard Medical School (HMS)
- **Assistant Professor** (조교수), Dept. of Plastic & Reconstructive Surgery, Chungnam National University College of Medicine

## Featured Open Source Projects
- **SolarLess Navi**: Experimental solar-aware smart navigation system comparing Fastest, Glare-Avoidance, and Shade-Priority routes using MapLibre GL 3D vector maps, NREL SPA, and precalculated 2.5D building/terrain occlusion across USA and Korea (154K+ tiles). [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/HyeokjaeKwon26/Solarless-Navi)
- **My Travel Diary**: 100% offline, privacy-first cinematic travel story reconstructor & video creation studio for Android with video clip editing, audio mixing, and 1080p MP4 export. [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/HyeokjaeKwon26/My-Travel-Diary)
- **My Travel Diary 3D**: Offline 3D globe & USGS DEM terrain travel reconstructor featuring OpenGL ES 2.0 meshes, physics-based 3D vehicles, cinematic chase cameras, and hardware-accelerated video export. [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/HyeokjaeKwon26/My-Travel-Diary-3D)
